# Report plan
[toc]

## Requirements

* use normal margins and 11-point Arial/Calibri font.
* File names should include your name and a shortened version of your project title.

## Abstract

Think of this like a brief summary of your research so in a few sentences describe the investigation and the main results. The reader will use this as a quick way to find out what your report is about.

---

## Introduction 

(including aim(s))

This should be a summary of the information found when carrying out research into the topic in addition to clearly setting out what is trying to be achieved with this investigation. This section should include everything that the reader needs to know in order to understand the investigation. Include references to other work from a variety of sources that your report is building on or links to.

Qs to ask yourself: What did you set out to do? What were you being asked to do? What information about the subject did you already know (for example, from research papers or articles)? Did you read up on the subject? If so, where did you find the information? How did you go about finding information (perhaps your project supervisor gave you some pointers about where to start)?

---

## Methodology

This is where you describe the steps taken throughout the investigation. The content of this section will depend on whether existing data was used or if experiments were conducted and data collected.

* If experimental work was carried out describe the design of the experiment.
* If data was analysed, describe the source of the data and what the data is. For example, this could be satellite data of a specific geographical area for a limited period of time.

Whatever the method, this section should be focussed around describing how research aims are being achieved. Feel free to include diagrams, graphs and/or photos because an image can enhance a piece of text in your report by helping to better communicate ideas.

*Qs to ask yourself: What sort of apparatus/equipment/techniques did you use and, in doing so, what safety precautions and special measures did* *you need to take and why?*

---

## Results and Discussion

In this section provide information about the results and what they mean. This can be presented in the form of charts, tables or graphs with accompanying explanatory text. If working with a large amount of numerical data, then it can be particularly helpful to think about different ways of presenting the results and perhaps including only some of the data rather than all of it.

*Qs to ask yourself: What results did you get? Were they what was expected or were they surprising? Do they support or contradict other* investigations or research on this topic?

----

## Conclusion

This should be a short summary that describes the results and links them back to the investigation aims.

*Qs to ask yourself: If the work was to continue, what needs to be done and is there anything further anyone should look at or pursue? If so, what is recommended? Could anything have been done differently to the way you did it?*

---

## Appendix

The appendix can contain any additional information that the reader might find interesting.
